Acharya Prashant was born Prashant Tripathi in Agra. He studied engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi and management at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad, and by his own account passed the civil services examination in 2000. Accounts differ on how he left that career, and Penguin's author interview says he worked for a time as a corporate consultant. He has called the move to teaching "a process of elevation and not of renunciation." He founded the Adwait Life-Education Foundation in 2006, and in 2015 the PrashantAdvait Foundation in Greater Noida, whose reach has come mostly through YouTube and a structured online programme on the Gita and the Upanishads.

He teaches Advaita Vedanta as psychology. The ego, the sense of being a separate doer and enjoyer, is in his account a false centre that acts out of fear and greed and suffers for it. Acting rightly, he says, "simply means to not act from a personal centre of greed and/or fear." He draws a sharp line between the ritual portions of the Vedas and Vedanta, reads the Gita's criticism of rites performed for reward as a rejection of religion used as a transaction, and rereads words such as karma, maya and dharma against their popular meanings. Veganism and climate action follow, in his teaching, from self-knowledge.

**Lineage.** He claims no guru and no initiation, so the tree connects him only to Advaita Vedanta as a tradition of texts.

**Practice.** Study of the Gita and the Upanishads through recorded talks, monthly live sessions and examinations.
