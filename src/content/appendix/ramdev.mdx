Ramdev was born Ram Kisan Yadav in Alipur, a village in Haryana's Mahendragarh district, to farming parents. The date usually given is 25 December 1965, though his birth year is uncertain. Reading *Satyarth Prakash*, the founding text of Dayananda Saraswati's Arya Samaj, turned him to gurukul schooling in Sanskrit and the Vedic texts, and in these years he met Balkrishna, his lifelong partner in business and Ayurveda. In the early 1990s he took the vows of a sannyasi from Swami Shankar Dev at Kripalu Bagh Ashram in Kankhal, Haridwar, and the name Ramdev. In 1995 he, Balkrishna and his yoga teacher Acharya Karamveer founded the Divya Yog Mandir Trust. From 2003 the early-morning yoga slot on Aastha TV made him a household figure, a saffron-robed monk leading stadium-sized crowds through breathing exercises. In 2006 he and Balkrishna founded Patanjali Ayurved.

He presents yoga as the system of Patanjali, a complete science of body, mind and spirit, and in practice places almost all the stress on the breath. He has long said that regular pranayama brings health, and he joins it to a programme of national renewal through a disciplined, vegetarian and self-reliant India. His originality lies in delivery, a fixed routine taught to a mass audience by television, and he added little to yoga's philosophy.

**Lineage.** He took sannyasa from Swami Shankar Dev and learned yoga from Acharya Karamveer; his education links him to Dayananda Saraswati's Arya Samaj.

**Practice.** A daily set of seven breathing exercises done at home in under an hour, above all *anulom vilom*, alternate-nostril breathing, and *kapalbhati*, sharp forced exhalations from the belly.
