Nithyananda was born Arunachalam Rajasekaran in Tiruvannamalai, the temple town at the foot of the hill Arunachala, on 1 January 1978 in most accounts. His organisation says he trained as a mechanical engineer and had a spiritual awakening as a boy, and he is reported to have joined the Ramakrishna Math in Chennai in 1995. In 2003 he founded the Nithyananda Dhyanapeetam at Bidadi, near Bengaluru, and in 2006 the Life Bliss Foundation in California. His discourses in Tamil and English, carried on television and YouTube, drew a large following among Tamils in India and abroad. In 2012 the elderly pontiff of the Madurai Aadheenam, an old Shaiva monastery, named him its 293rd head, an appointment withdrawn the same year.

He presents himself as an enlightened master and, in his organisation's words, an avatar of what it calls superconsciousness. He draws on the Agamas, the ritual scriptures of Shiva worship, and his programmes promise to awaken *Shivoham*, the realisation "I am Shiva." His organisation has claimed powers of healing for him, and in an interview he played the claims down: "I teach only meditation, yogic kriyas." In December 2019 he announced Kailasa, which his followers describe as a sovereign, borderless nation for Hindus, with its own flag and passport. The religious content is conventional Shaiva devotion and guru worship; the declared nation is the new element.

**Lineage.** He names no single human guru whose line he continues; his documented tie to Tamil Shaivism runs through the brief Madurai appointment and the Agamic worship of his temples.

**Practice.** Meditation programmes such as the residential Inner Awakening course and daily satsangs streamed online, joined to Shaiva temple ritual.
