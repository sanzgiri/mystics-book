## Life

Dhirendra Krishna Garg was born on 4 July 1996 in Gada, a village in Chhatarpur district in the Bundelkhand region of Madhya Pradesh. His father, Ram Kripal Garg, was a priest, and his mother is Saroj Garg. Accounts describe a poor Brahmin household. As a schoolboy in nearby Ganj he recited kathas to villagers. The family served a small Hanuman shrine at Gada, Bageshwar Dham, which they say was founded by his ancestor Sannyasi Baba, described in some accounts as his great-grandfather. Reports describe Dhirendra as the third generation of the family to lead it, after his grandfather and father.

He became known as Dhirendra Krishna Shastri, and his devotees call him Bageshwar Dham Sarkar or Bageshwar Baba. He describes himself as a disciple of Jagadguru Rambhadracharya, the scholar-monk of Chitrakoot. From the late 2010s videos of his twice-weekly darbars circulated widely on YouTube and devotional television, and by 2023 he was drawing very large crowds to katha programmes across north India and abroad. Wikipedia counted about 7.5 million followers across his social media accounts.

The shrine has grown with his fame. It runs a free kitchen, holds an annual mass wedding for brides from poor families and is building a Vedic school. On 23 February 2025 Prime Minister Narendra Modi laid the foundation stone of the Bageshwar Dham Medical and Science Research Institute, a cancer hospital planned on ten acres at Gada with a first phase of 100 beds and free treatment for poor patients. Modi called Shastri "my younger brother Dhirendra Shastri."

## The teaching

Shastri's teaching is devotion to Hanuman, the servant of Rama, whom he calls Bageshwar Balaji, and to Rama through the *Ramcharitmanas* of Tulsidas. He presents himself as a servant of the deity. "I am just a servant at the feet of Bageshwar Balaji," he said in 2023 when his powers were questioned. His kathas mix scripture, songs and moral instruction on family life, vegetarianism and loyalty to Sanatan Dharma, his preferred name for Hinduism.

The divya darbar, or divine court, held every Tuesday and Saturday, is what made him famous. Visitors register for a token, and a few are called to the stage. Shastri writes on a slip of paper, the *parcha*, what he says is the visitor's problem, often as soon as he looks at them, and sometimes announces names and family details aloud. Devotees take this as proof that he knows their troubles without being told. He also gives remedies for illness, family trouble and what devotees describe as possession by spirits. He says the knowledge comes from Hanuman, from his own sadhana or spiritual discipline, and from the blessing of Sannyasi Baba. "I have faith in my God," he told reporters in January 2023.

He has preached against caste division among Hindus. In November 2024 he led a 160-kilometre, nine-day Sanatan Hindu Ekta Padyatra, a unity march, from Bageshwar Dham to Orchha. "The purpose of this yatra is to unite Hindus by removing the caste system and the animosity that divides us," he said at its start. A second march, from Delhi to Vrindavan, followed in November 2025.

## Lineage

<MiniTree id="dhirendra-shastri" />

Shastri's authority rests on two lines. The first is family: the shrine and its powers, as he tells it, descend from **Sannyasi Baba** through his grandfather and father, a devotional claim. The second is his discipleship to **Jagadguru Rambhadracharya**, a documented relationship that ties him to the Ramanandi Vaishnava world of Rama devotion.

## Critiques and questions

**The Nagpur challenge, 2023.** In January 2023, while Shastri was holding a katha in Nagpur, Shyam Manav of the Akhil Bharatiya Andhashraddha Nirmoolan Samiti, a rationalist group that campaigns against superstition, offered him Rs 30 lakh if he could correctly name ten people chosen by the group, with their phone numbers and other details. Shastri answered that he did not operate behind closed doors and invited critics to come and watch on camera. In April 2026, during a second katha in Nagpur, Shastri again invited doubters, and Manav raised his offer to Rs 80 lakh. The reports available do not record that the test was ever held.

**How the readings work.** According to *DailyO*, visitors who want to meet him first take a token through the shrine's website, giving their own, their father's and their children's names and a mobile number. At a darbar in Raipur on 20 January 2023 Shastri named relatives of an ABP News reporter, Gyanendra Tiwari, and the channel aired it as a live miracle. The fact-checking site BOOM found that the relatives' names and the family housewarming he mentioned were on the reporter's public Facebook profile, and an ABP News executive told BOOM, "The information is available on Facebook, there is nothing astonishing about this." Shastri says his knowledge comes from Balaji and invites anyone to test him on camera.
