## Life

Nithyananda was born Arunachalam Rajasekaran in Tiruvannamalai, the temple town at the foot of the hill Arunachala, in Tamil Nadu. Most accounts give his birth date as 1 January 1978; some give 1977. His own organisation says he trained as a mechanical engineer and that he had a spiritual awakening as a boy. He is reported to have joined the Ramakrishna Math in Chennai in 1995, under the influence of his grandfather.

In 2003 he founded the Nithyananda Dhyanapeetam, an ashram at Bidadi on the Bengaluru–Mysuru road, and in 2006 the Life Bliss Foundation in California. His discourses, given in Tamil and English and spread on television and YouTube, drew a large following among Tamils at home and abroad, and his centres multiplied in India and the United States. In 2012 he was named the 293rd head of the Madurai Aadheenam, an old Shaiva monastery, by its elderly pontiff. The appointment was withdrawn the same year. In 2013 he received the title Mahamandaleshwar.

In 2019 he left India. In December 2019 he announced Kailasa, which he and his followers describe as a sovereign state for Hindus. His whereabouts are not publicly known. On 1 April 2025, after reports on social media, attributed to a relative, that he had died, Kailasa issued a statement saying he was alive, healthy and active.

## The teaching

Nithyananda presents himself as an enlightened master and, in his organisation's words, as an avatar, a living incarnation of what it calls superconsciousness. He draws on Shaiva tradition and the Agamas, the ritual scriptures of Shiva worship, and his broadcast programmes promise to awaken *Shivoham*, the realisation "I am Shiva." Later he took the name Nithyananda Paramashivam, and Kailasa's flag shows Paramashiva, the supreme Shiva, with the bull Nandi and Nithyananda seated below.

His method combines meditation programmes, such as the residential Inner Awakening course and daily satsangs streamed online, with temple ritual and claims of powers. He and his organisation have said he can heal illness, open a person's third eye, and make animals speak. In an interview he played these claims down. "I teach only meditation, yogic kriyas," he said in an interview, describing his healing as help for psychosomatic disorders.

Kailasa extends the teaching into politics. Its representatives describe it as a revival of "the enlightened Hindu civilisation," a borderless nation for persecuted Hindus run through non-profit bodies, with its own passport, flag and a Reserve Bank of Kailasa, launched in August 2020.

## Lineage

<MiniTree id="nithyananda" />

Nithyananda names no single human guru whose line he continues. His reported time at the **Ramakrishna Math** is not independently documented. His tie to **Tamil Shaivism** is documented through the brief Madurai Aadheenam appointment of 2012 and the Agamic worship of his temples.

## Critiques and questions

**Kailasa's recognition.** No government or international body recognises Kailasa. On 22 and 24 February 2023, a woman calling herself Kailasa's permanent ambassador, Vijayapriya Nithyananda, took part in meetings of two UN committees in Geneva. The UN human rights office said one submission was "irrelevant to the topic of discussion" and the other "tangential to the topic at hand," and would not be considered. She then said that Kailasa "respects India as its Gurupeedam." Newark, New Jersey, which had signed a sister-city agreement with Kailasa in January 2023, rescinded it a week later.
