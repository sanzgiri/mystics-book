# Notes per entry (read only the lines for your entries)

## Part 1 · Reason and Revelation
- rammohan-roy, dayananda-saraswati, debendranath-tagore, keshab-chandra-sen: focus on how they reread scripture, their view of God, revelation, image worship, the Vedas and reason; social reform only where it expresses these ideas.
- jyotirao-phule (sidebar): the anti-caste counter-voice of the period.

## Part 2 · Neo-Vedanta
- ramakrishna: his many sadhanas (Kali, Bhairavi Brahmani's tantra, Totapuri's Advaita, Islam, Christianity) and the evidence for each; the Gospel of Sri Ramakrishna and "M"; scholarly debates (Jeffrey Kripal's Kali's Child and responses) handled fairly.
- vivekananda: Practical Vedanta, the four yogas, the 1893 Parliament, his reframing of Hinduism as universal and experiential; debates about how much was Ramakrishna's and how much his own.
- chandrasekharendra-saraswati: the Kanchi Paramacharya (1894–1994) as the orthodox voice of Shankara's tradition; his discourses (Deivathin Kural / Hindu Dharma); his positions on varnashrama and tradition stated fairly; the debate over Kanchi's claim to be one of Shankara's original mathas, both sides.
- rama-tirtha: maternal uncle of Papaji (see papaji.mdx).
- sarvapriyananda, acharya-prashant: living; only well-documented public facts.
- radhakrishnan: his philosophy, not his presidency.
- dayananda-arsha: Swami Dayananda Saraswati of Arsha Vidya (1930–2015), not the Arya Samaj founder.

## Part 4 · Evolution and the Integral

- iqbal: khudi and The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam; politics stated factually without judgement.
- kc-bhattacharya: "Svaraj in Ideas", "The Subject as Freedom".
- gopi-krishna: his kundalini account and evolutionary theory.

## Part 7 · Love and Grace

- amma: living; darshan by embrace, love and selfless service as practice, Krishna and Devi bhavas attributed; criticism (Gail Tredwell's 2013 Holy Hell and the ashram's response; skeptic critiques) precisely and fairly.
- bhaktivinoda: reframed Gaudiya Vaishnavism for educated Bengalis; the Mayapur birthplace identification and its disputes.
- bhaktisiddhanta: Gaudiya Math, preaching mission, sannyasa reforms.
- shastriji-pramukh: BAPS line, Shastriji's 1907 founding, Akshar-Purushottam doctrine, Yogiji Maharaj briefly, Pramukh Swami; Mahant Swami as node. Theology first.
- papa-ramdas: Anandashram, Ram nam, In Quest of God.
- neem-karoli-baba: Hanuman devotion; Ram Dass's Be Here Now and Miracle of Love; miracle stories as devotees' accounts.
- premanand-maharaj: living Radhavallabh saint in Vrindavan; only well-documented facts.
