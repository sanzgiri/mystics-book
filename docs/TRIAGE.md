# Issue triage

Generated by `scripts/triage-issues.py` from `docs/OPEN_ISSUES_BATCHES.md` (838 items). Ranking is by keyword heuristic, so it errs toward the higher tier; the reconcile sweep re-reads each item against the current text.

## Policy

Every item ends in one of three states:

1. **Verified:** a reliable source was read and the text matches it. Item closed.
2. **Stated as unverified:** the claim stays only if it matters to the argument, and the text carries its attribution ("by his own account", "according to X", "the date is disputed") or says plainly that it could not be confirmed.
3. **Removed:** the claim does not matter to the argument and cannot be verified. It is cut.

Living people and legal matters follow a stricter rule: if a statement is not well documented by at least one reliable primary or scholarly source, it is cut, and any response from the person or organisation is given.

## Strategy

1. **Reconcile sweep, Tier 1 first** (Opus, because these are legal and living-person claims). For each file, read the text and each issue. Close issues the checker already fixed; for the rest, try one more verification and then apply the policy above.
2. **Tier 2:** same sweep, Sonnet 5.5 with a fetch budget per file; quotations that cannot be seen verbatim are paraphrased with attribution or cut.
3. **Tier 3 and 4:** one automated pass. Where the text already hedges, close the item. Where a single-source date matters, give it as "c." or a range or attribute it. Spelling and coordinates are fixed in bulk.
4. **Author decisions** (title, voice, verdict lines, photos, items flagged "your decision") are listed in `docs/OPEN_ISSUES.md` for the pre-launch review and are not worked by agents.
5. A closing audit counts what is left. Anything open at launch appears on the site as a visible "sources and confidence" note, not as a silent assumption.
