# Sourcing log, group 1 (7 Nov 2026)

Method note: only WebSearch and WebFetch were available; Open Library, WorldCat and JSTOR were not reachable. Each added work was confirmed against a library catalogue record or publisher page (author, title, publisher, year) fetched in this session. Where a catalogue gave a reprint year only, the original date already in the chapter was kept and the reprint labelled. No page numbers added. Primary texts of the figures were not readable online here, so no existing quotation was re-checked against its source.

## rammohan-roy
Added: Zastoupil (Palgrave Macmillan 2010); Robertson (OUP Delhi 1995); Hatcher (Harvard UP 2020); K. W. Jones, New Cambridge History of India III.1 (CUP 1989); Salmond (Laurier UP 2004).
Confirmed/corrected: none of the chapter's claims could be checked against these books (not read). No text changes.
Weak: first Brahmo Sabha meeting and order of service (Wikipedia), trust deed text (Wikipedia), Tibet journey and sister-in-law story (already flagged in the chapter), Tuhfat date.

## dayananda-saraswati
Added: Dalmia (OUP Delhi 1997); Jones (CUP 1989); Lajpat Rai, The Arya Samaj (Longmans 1915). Publisher/place added to Jordens (OUP 1978), Salmond (Laurier UP 2004), Jones Arya Dharm (UC Press 1976).
Weak: 1869 Kashi debate (Wikipedia "Shastrartha"), Theosophical Society link (Theosophy Wiki), Sanaullah Amritsari / Haq Prakash (Wikipedia), Shivratri account (Arya Samaj site).

## ramakrishna
Added: Sarkar essay in Writing Social History (OUP 1997); Jackson, Vedanta for the West (Indiana UP 1994); Beckerlegge (OUP, 2000 per Open University record; some reviews date it 2001). Saradananda edition detail added (Sri Ramakrishna Math, Mylapore, 1952, 2nd rev. ed.).
Weak: Wikipedia-only items (Views on Ramakrishna, Kathamrita); Sil, Rolland, Isherwood, Max Muller left as cited, not re-verified here.

## vivekananda
Added: Sharma (Yale UP 2013); Sen (OUP 2000); Jackson (1994); Beckerlegge (2000). Place/publisher confirmed for Rambachan (Hawaii 1994); Medhananda (OUP 2022) confirmed.
Weak: Kanyakumari resolve and chronology rest on Wikipedia (refs 17-18); voice-recording claims on Belur Math/BOOM pages (acceptable, organisational).

## narayana-guru
Added: Jeffrey (Sussex UP 1976); Chandramohan (Tulika 2016); Sanal Mohan (OUP 2015); Osella and Osella (Pluto 2000); Nitya Chaitanya Yati, That Alone (D. K. Printworld, 2nd ed. 2013), also added to the "one book" pick.
Weak: much of the chapter rests on Kerala Tourism, SNGM and Wikipedia (archived) pages and a magazine report on the Tagore meeting; none confirmed in the new scholarship (not read). A Complete Works edition (Muni Narayana Prasad) was found but publisher and year could not be verified, so it is not cited.

## chandrasekharendra-saraswati
Added: Fuller and Narasimhan (Chicago 2014). Detail added: Mahadevan, Preceptors of Advaita (Madras 1968); Cenkner (Motilal, 1983, reprint 2001 confirmed); Voice of God publisher (Kanchi Mahaswami Peetarohana Shatabdi Mahotsava Trust, from a bookseller listing).
Weak: only one new scholarly source found. The antiquity dispute rests on Wikipedia, an Indology list post and Veeramani (partisan); Sundaresan's 1999 list post (read) agrees that the Dasanami records lack a Kanchi matha, unchanged text. Brunton chapter, Hinduism Today pieces left as is.

## ramana-maharshi
Added: Forsthoefel (Ashgate 2002); Lucas, Nova Religio 17:3 (2014); Godman, Be As You Are (Arkana 1985, Penguin 1989 ed.). Detail added: Collected Works (Weiser 1997), Talks (Sri Ramanasramam 2006 ed.), Osborne and Brunton reprints.
Weak: 1896 death-experience account and Nan Yar? history rest on Godman's and James's web pages and the Narasimha Swami biography (not seen). Mudaliar, Day by Day, found but publisher unverified, not added.

## nisargadatta
Added: Lucas (2014); Ranade, Mysticism in Maharashtra (Poona 1933, confirmed from a Cambridge review record); Acorn Press 1988 edition of I Am That noted.
Weak: scholarly literature on him is thin. Dates, initiation, the Nimbargi/Bhausaheb/Navnath lineage and the 1936 and 1951 events rest on Wikipedia, Conway and ashram sites; not confirmed.

## papaji
Added: Lucas (2014); Talks with Sri Ramana Maharshi (2006 ed.); Be As You Are (1985/1989). Godman, Nothing Ever Happened: Avadhuta Foundation confirmed as publisher (davidgodman.org); the 1998 date is the chapter's own and is not confirmed on that page.
Weak: nearly everything rests on Godman (a devotee-biographer) and Avadhuta pages; mining years and the 1944 meeting unconfirmed independently. The Cohen entries are Wikipedia only.

## aurobindo
Added: Heehs, Brief Biography (OUP India Paperbacks 1997); Heehs, Bomb in Bengal (OUP 1993); Phillips (Brill 1986); Purani (Ashram, 4th ed. 1978). Heehs, Lives (Columbia 2008) confirmed.
Weak: Savitri structure, Nobel nominations and Pavitra letter rest on Wikipedia/Overman Foundation and ashram sites.
