# Sourcing log, group 2

Method note: the sandbox allowed web search and page fetch but not catalogue APIs. Each added work was confirmed to exist through publisher, library or catalogue pages returned by search; author, title and publisher were read on a page for: Mallinson (Routledge page), Alter (Princeton page), Gurus of Modern Yoga (contents), Gurus in America (MUSE contents; Humes chapter via her CV), Sanderson Shaiva Age (Zenodo), Sanderson Festschrift (OAPEN), Rochford (UBC Press listing), Brown & Gerbarg (abstract page), Kjellgren (PMC), Newcombe (OU repository), Sawyer & Humes (CUP front matter), Dimock/Stewart (Indology list). Others (Foxen 2020, De Michelis, Singh, Muller-Ortega, Dyczkowski, Williamson, Goldberg American Veda, Tamal Krishna Goswami, Shinn, Bryant, Mallinson & Singleton) were confirmed as catalogue/bookseller records in search results; years are those shown there. No page numbers added except where seen (Humes 55-79; Sanderson 41-350; Brown & Gerbarg 189-201). No chapter prose changed. No corrections.

All additions are in a new "Further scholarly reading" group at the end of each References list, numbering continued.

## lahiri-mahasaya
Added: Mallinson 2007 (Khecarividya); Mallinson & Singleton 2017; Foxen 2017; Goldberg 2018; Yukteswar, Holy Science (SRF 1977 ed., archive.org).
Confirmed: khechari mudra and the spinal/breath techniques predate Lahiri in hatha texts (Mallinson).
Still weak: Babaji meeting and date (lineage and Wikipedia only); diaries (Chatterjee only); Moradabad episode; photograph story; Panchanan details.

## yogananda
Added: Foxen 2020; Goldberg, American Veda 2010; Yukteswar; Newcombe 2009; De Michelis 2004.
Pick kept (Autobiography; Goldberg biography named).
Still weak: SRF-site chronology items (refs 9-15) and the Boston Globe film review; Wikipedia.

## krishnamacharya
Added: Singleton & Fraser chapter (Gurus of Modern Yoga); Alter 2004; De Michelis 2004; Newcombe 2009; KYM site.
Pick kept (Singleton).
Still weak: Yoga Korunta/Calcutta library story (Steiner web page); Ricci and Ruiz Yoga Journal pieces.

## sivananda
Added: Newcombe 2009; De Michelis 2004; Alter 2004; Singleton 2010, all as context. I could not confirm that they discuss Sivananda in detail, so none is cited as confirming a Sivananda claim.
Still weak: nearly all biographical facts rest on the Divine Life Society and disciples' texts (Autobiography, Venkatesananda); Wikipedia for Satchidananda.

## maharishi
Added: Humes 2005; Sawyer & Humes 2023 (Cambridge Element, not open access); Williamson 2010; Goldberg 2010.
Still weak: Maharishi's birth year and early life (Mason site, Britannica/EBSCO summaries); finances (NPR); Beatles items (Beatles Bible).

## sri-sri-ravi-shankar
Added: Waghorne chapter; Brown & Gerbarg 2005; Kjellgren et al. 2007 (open access). Only three; I found no further verifiable scholarly work on him. A Equinox article "Art of Living: Religious Entrepreneurship and Legitimation Strategies" (IJSNR 2:2) exists but its author could not be read, so it was not added.
Still weak: early life and 1982/1984 Sudarshan Kriya origin (Art of Living site, Wikipedia, Reader's Digest via Wikipedia); Gautier biography is sympathetic.

## sadhguru
Added: Waghorne chapter only. No other scholarly source found and verified.
Still weak: the 1982 experience and "lifetimes" (Isha and authorised biography); Wikipedia; press items.

## lakshmanjoo
Added: Hughes, Self Realization in Kashmir Shaivism (SUNY, 1994); Dyczkowski 1987; Muller-Ortega 1989; Singh, Pratyabhijnahrdayam; Sanderson, Shaiva Age (2009, OA); Sanderson Festschrift (Brill 2020, OA).
Unverified existing item: ref 12 (Sanderson chapter on Lakshman Joo in Samvidullasah, 2007). The volume exists (D.K. Printworld, Bäumer and Kumar), but I could not confirm Sanderson's contribution. Left unchanged; please check.
Weak: biography (Academy, Raina, Hinduism Today); the claim of unbroken lineage is already flagged in the chapter.

## muktananda
Added: Jain chapter (Gurus of Modern Yoga); Williamson 2010; Goldberg 2010; Muller-Ortega 1989.
Still weak: early life and the 1947 initiation (Siddha Yoga sources and Muktananda's own book); dates in Wikipedia-derived items.

## prabhupada
Added: Dimock & Stewart (HOS 56; year given as 1999, released 2000, not confirmed on a page); Tamal Krishna Goswami 2012; Rochford 1985; Shinn 1987; Bryant 2007.
Still weak: ref 10 (Rosen, p. 220, Madhva affiliation) and refs 9 (Burt, Doktorski) were left untouched and unverified; Vedabase/Vanipedia items are devotional.
